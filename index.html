Ethically, intellectually, and creatively competent

ROSE: A Values-Based Decision-Maker

  • EthicallyROSE is built on your values. You name who your work serves, and credit and compensate the people who shape it.
  • IntellectuallyROSE is built on theory and quantitative modeling, with math you can check.
  • CreativelyROSE is a live coach and toolkit. Every idea and final decision comes from you; ROSE guides you, provides the formula, and reveals your values and your product's utility.

ROSE is a mindfulness-based app that self-informs quantitative decision-making, scoring outcomes on self-satisfaction, self-concept, projected success rate, and life flourishing.

For everyday people, consumers, creators, professionals, and scientists; individuals and organizations
